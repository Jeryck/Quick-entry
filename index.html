New entries are sent automatically when you’re online. The manual copy options below are a fallback.

Send to your budget sheet

Copy a block, open the Transactions tab in Google Sheets, tap the first empty cell of that block and paste. Then tap “Mark as sent”.

Backup

Your data lives only on this phone. Save a backup file regularly (for example to Files or iCloud Drive).

Connect Google Sheet

Paste the web app address and the key from your sheet’s script. They are saved only on this phone. Don’t share them. When connected, your names come from the sheet.

Install on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari while online.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it once from the Home Screen. After that it works offline.
